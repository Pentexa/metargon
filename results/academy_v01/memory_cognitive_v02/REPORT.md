## 5. Verdict — inconclusive for improvement; exploration protected

**Primary success criterion (meaningful improvement over epoch_reset) is NOT
met.** v02 ties epoch_reset on classic frozen and loses modestly on
conflict/noisy/OOD frozen (deltas −0.04…−0.16; all 95% CIs overlap zero)
and on classic/conflict adaptive; it wins on noisy adaptive (+0.11) and on
retention (+0.125 vs −0.188, both seeds positive).

**However, the two historical failure modes are fixed:**

- A/B/A repeated learning: v02 ≡ epoch_reset (A1=1.0, A2=1.0, A2−A1=0.0)
  on both seeds — the v0.1 collapse (A2=0.125) is gone.
- Exploration: exact novel-single discovery is undamaged (0.364 exact rate
  vs epoch_reset 0.375), confirming the cap preserves exploration slots.

Mechanism ablations are coherent: v02_gate_only (no trusted-transfer tier)
degenerates frozen classic behavior to ~fresh (no reuse), which is why the
trusted-transfer tier is necessary for transfer parity; v02_nocap (cap
off) is the only variant with a significant noisy-frozen gain (+0.084
[+0.014, +0.167]) but loses OOD; v02_nogate (no active gate) loses conflict
significantly. No ablation beats epoch_reset overall.

**Conclusion:** v0.2 is a safe, non-harmful mechanism (evidence preserved,
checkpoints intact, exploration protected, A/B/A recovered, retention
improved) but does not demonstrate the targeted improvement over
`epoch_reset` on the measured transfer tasks. Reported honestly; no forced
positive. Default-off behavior is preserved; `CognitiveMemoryV02` is
available for future work.
